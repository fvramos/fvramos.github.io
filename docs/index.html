Fernando Ramos

Associate Professor at Instituto Superior Técnico

Senior Researcher at INESC-ID, member of DPSS

IST·INESC-ID·DPSS·Google Scholar

I'm a computer scientist interested in how we design, program, and accelerate networks and systems. My research spans Internet architecture, programmable data planes, network security, and the increasingly exciting intersection of AI/ML and networking—in both directions. I enjoy pushing the boundaries of what networks can do, especially when it makes them more flexible, secure, and blazingly fast.

Biography

Fernando Ramos is an Associate Professor at Instituto Superior Técnico, University of Lisbon, and a senior researcher at INESC-ID. His research interests include software-defined networking, network programmability and acceleration, network security, and machine learning for networks.

He holds a PhD from the University of Cambridge and an MSc from Queen Mary University of London. His previous academic and research experience includes the Faculty of Sciences of the University of Lisbon, the University of Cambridge, Telefónica Research in Barcelona, and Altice Labs.

He coordinates the Master’s Programme in Telecommunications and Informatics Engineering, and served as Scientific Coordinator of the Distributed, Parallel and Secure Systems (DPSS) research area at INESC-ID from 2022 to 2025. He is co-chair of the P4 Education Working Group.
